What we heard

The brief

Thailand's provinces, districts, subdistricts and postal codes for developers, checked against official sources. The mark should say place and precision, feel Thai-made, and stay clear of the map-pin-on-blue look of every other map brand.

precisecheckedstructuredThai-madeopen

Recognisable at 16 px, the same hand as the wordmark, distinct from map brands and the old logo, works in one colour.

GeoThai — identity concepts2
Concept A

G-pin

The wordmark's own G, its lower curve drawn out into the tip of a location pin.

  • One hand: the symbol is the G of Bai Jamjuree, the wordmark's typeface
  • Says place at a glance, without a globe or a map of Thailand
  • Laterite red-brown owns a warm colour in a blue category
